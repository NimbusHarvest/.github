# ☁️ Nimbus Harvest

An immersive iOS idle farming game where your floating island ecosystem adapts to the real-world weather outside your window. 

---

## 📖 About The Project

**Nimbus Harvest** reimagines the idle farming genre for iOS. Set on a tranquil floating island in the sky, your agricultural empire is directly tied to the physical world. By integrating real-time meteorological data, the game creates a dynamic environment where your actual local climate, temperature, and atmospheric conditions dictate your digital harvest, crop growth rates, and market fluctuations.

### ✨ Key Features

*   **🌍 Live Weather Synchronization:** Your floating island’s climate mirrors the real-world weather of your physical location.
*   **📈 Dynamic Forecast Economy:** Crop yields and market prices shift based on temperature and weather patterns. Check your local forecast to plan the ultimate farming strategy.
*   **🏝️ Expand Your Sky Sanctuary:** Unlock, upgrade, and customize your floating island as your operation grows.
*   **⏳ Progress on the Go:** A relaxing idle progression system that keeps your farm thriving even when the app is closed.
